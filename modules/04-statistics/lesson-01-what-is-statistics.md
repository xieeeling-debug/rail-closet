# 04.1 — What Statistics Is (and Is Not)

## You will learn

- Why software engineers need stats basics  
- Population vs sample  
- Data types  

## Why this module exists

Apps run on **data**: clicks, prices, clothing tags, quiz scores.  
Statistics helps you:

- Summarize (“average session is 3 minutes”)  
- Compare (“tops sell more than shoes”)  
- Avoid fooling yourself with noisy numbers  

## Population vs sample

- **Population** — every member you care about (all users, all clothes you own)  
- **Sample** — a subset you actually measured  

Example: You cannot survey every shopper on Earth; you survey 200 and estimate.

## Data types (practical)

| Kind | Examples | Typical ops |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Numeric | price, age, score | mean, sum |
| Categorical | color, type, city | counts, percentages |
| Boolean | is_raining | True/False rates |

## Statistics is not magic

Stats **summarizes and estimates**. It does not automatically prove causation.

“People who buy umbrellas also buy boots” does not mean umbrellas cause boot purchases — both relate to rain.
