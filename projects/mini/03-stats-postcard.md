# Mini M3 — Stats Postcard

## Goal

One-page Markdown “postcard” summarizing a tiny dataset.

## Requirements

- Dataset ≥ 15 values (CSV or list)  
- Mean, median, range, and a frequency table or stdev  
- Text bar chart  
- `postcard.md` written for a non-technical friend  

## Stretch

- Compare two groups (e.g. weekday vs weekend study minutes)
